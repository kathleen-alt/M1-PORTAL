# Competitive Positioning

How Orca Coast wins against larger indoor-playground manufacturers. (Competitor
profiles below are based on public positioning; their sites block automated
fetching, so treat specifics as directional and verify before quoting.)

## The two named competitors

### iPlayco (International Play Company) — Surrey, BC
- **What they are:** one of the largest, longest-established global indoor-play
  manufacturers; full catalog (soft play, themed, climbers, trampoline, sport).
- **Strengths:** scale, global brand, breadth of product, big-budget references,
  heavy trade-show presence (IAAPA), distributor reach.
- **Where there's an opening:** scale tends to mean standardized processes,
  longer lead times, and a reliance on **inbound** (web inquiries, dealers,
  booths). Large players rarely run **proactive, regional outbound**.

### Atomic Playgrounds — US
- **What they are:** custom/themed indoor-playground designer-manufacturer,
  strong creative/themed builds for FECs and churches.
- **Strengths:** themed design flair, custom projects.
- **Where there's an opening:** narrower vertical focus and, again, inbound-led
  demand generation.

## Orca Coast's differentiators (real, from the portfolio)
1. **Custom to ANY size & budget** — themed, toddler, and large-scale; you fit
   the space, not a catalog SKU.
2. **Turnkey: design + manufacture + install** — one partner, not a drop-ship.
3. **Proof across venue types** — 200+ completed projects in **CA + US** spanning
   recreation/community centres, churches, daycares, museums, aquariums, zoos,
   resorts, libraries, malls, and FECs. Few competitors can show that breadth of
   *reference projects* in a given category + region.
4. **Regional relationships & small/mid agility** — your portfolio is strongest
   exactly where big players are weakest: independent operators and small-to-mid
   towns.

## The structural edge: the Growth Engine
Bigger competitors **wait for inbound**. This platform makes Orca Coast
**proactive**:
- Finds **greenfield** family venues (don't have a playground yet) before they
  start shopping.
- **Lookalike-matches** each to a real Orca project, so every first touch carries
  category + regional proof ("we built one for a church just like yours in TX").
- Generates a **personalized, on-brand email** (now with the custom/turnkey
  differentiators baked in) and sequences it the same week.
- Times outreach to the right **trade shows** (see CONFERENCES.md).

**Net:** out-prospect, don't out-spend. Win the deal before the RFP by being the
first credible, specific, local-feeling option in the buyer's inbox.

## Messaging do/don't
- **Do:** lead with their families/mission; cite a specific similar project;
  stress custom-fit + turnkey + Canadian/US track record.
- **Don't:** name or knock competitors; compete on lowest price (compete on fit,
  proof, and partnership).
